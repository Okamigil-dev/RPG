# RPG

1. BODY: The Physical Plane
The "Hard" Stat. This is your physical mass, your speed, and your raw survivability.

Primary Logic: "Can I physically endure this or exert my will on the world?"

Skill Equivalents: Athletics, Acrobatics, Physical Stealth, and Biological Endurance.

Combat Roles:

Health: Determines your Max HP.

Power: Directs Melee Attack damage and Physical Defense.

Saving Throws: Used to resist being shoved, grappled, poisoned, or exhausted.

2. MIND: The Logical Plane
The "Precision" Stat. This is your efficiency, your observation, and your technical mastery of magic.

Primary Logic: "Can I find the most efficient way to solve this problem?"

Skill Equivalents: Perception, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Arcana, and History.

Combat & Magic Roles:

Spell Power: Directs how hard your magical effects hit.

Efficiency (The "Knowledge" Door): Higher MIND reduces the Mana Cost of spells and techniques.

Accuracy: Directs precision/ranged strikes.

Saving Throws: Used to resist illusions, sensory confusion, and mental traps.

3. SPIRIT: The Intuitive Plane
The "Energy" Stat. This is your soul's capacity, your social presence, and your willpower.

Primary Logic: "How deep is my reservoir of energy, and how strong is my presence?"

Skill Equivalents: Religion, Insight, Persuasion, Deception, and Intimidation.

Combat & Magic Roles:

Mana Pool: Determines your Max MP.

Sustainability: Controls your Regeneration Rates (HP & MP).

Saving Throws: Used to resist fear, soul-possession, mana-drain, and charms.
